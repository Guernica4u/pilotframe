# Titanfall 1, not Titanfall 2

Notes from the owner (2026-10-05) so we don't build TF2 features by mistake. Most online material (wikis, videos, mods) is about TF2, so check here first.

| Topic | Titanfall 1 (what we build) | Titanfall 2 (don't copy) |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign | **No single player.** Multiplayer only (plus the "campaign multiplayer" playlist). | Has a single-player campaign. |
| Grunts | **More voice lines.** Carry a **wider range of weapons, including anti-titan weapons.** | Fewer lines, smaller loadouts. |
| Burn cards | **Yes.** One-use perks picked before a spawn. | Replaced by Boosts. |
| Titans | **Fewer chassis (Atlas, Ogre, Stryder), built DIY**: you pick weapon, tactical, ordnance and kits yourself. | Premade titan classes (Ion, Scorch, etc.) with fixed kits. |

## HUD (from R1Delta, PC)

Reference: `Resources/reference/hud/pilot_hud_sidearm_r1delta.webp` (gitignored).

- The crosshair is a small 4-tick cross in the centre.
- The weapon panel is in the bottom right on an angled dark plate: the weapon name (e.g. "Hammond P2011"), the grenade count ("x0" with an icon), and the big magazine count underneath.
